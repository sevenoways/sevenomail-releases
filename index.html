Private by design

Your email is between you and your mail provider. Sevenomail stays out of it.

  • No tracking pixels. Images from the internet stay blocked until you choose to load them, so senders can't see when you open their mail.
  • No scripts. Emails are shown in a locked-down view that can't run code.
  • Links open in your browser, never inside your mail.
  • No Sevenomail account, no analytics. Mail travels directly between your PC and your mail server.
  • Passwords in Windows Credential Manager, never stored in a file.
  • Signed updates. Sevenomail asks before updating and checks every update's signature first.
